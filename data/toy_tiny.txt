Add visual trainer for weights and stepped epochs

ADD bridge.c
Read create/train/sample/values/state and reply with one JSON snapshot. cmd_create calls network_create_sizes for xor or one custom sample; cmd_train repeats set/forward/backward/update and loss_add; emit_state prints activations, biases, weights, and mse.

ADD server.py
Serve the page and hold one ann-gui child. command_for maps /api/create, /api/train, /api/sample, /api/values, and /api/state onto that line protocol.

ADD index.html
Lay out dataset, layer sizes, learning rate, seed, and steps with Launch/Step/Run/Play. Place the network canvas, loss canvas, weight strip, and sample rows.

ADD style.css
Style the network view black, blue for positive weights and red for negative, and body.flow as the light tile view.

ADD app.js
drawNet maps activation to brightness and weight sign to color; drawLoss and renderHeat show the trace and every matrix. doLaunch/doTrain/playLoop step the bridge, and a click lists one neuron's bias and weights.
